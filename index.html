⚠️ Data Limitation Notice
This layer shows accumulated rainfall estimated from IMERG Early Run satellite-microwave data (NASA GPM, V07C). IMERG Early Run is a near-real-time product with ~4-hour latency and is known to underestimate rainfall intensity, particularly over complex terrain and during convective events. Localised or orographic rainfall may not be fully captured. Treat as indicative only — validate against rain-gauge observations for water resource decisions.

DATA SOURCES
RTDAS Data — Real-time data from WRD Assam's RTDAS stations and IMD's AWS/ARG network across Northeast India are integrated into the system and updated every 15–60 minutes.
WRF (NESAC) — High-resolution regional weather forecast model run daily by the North Eastern Space Applications Centre.
GFS (NCEP) — Global Forecast System from NOAA/NCEP, USA, providing district-level rainfall and temperature outlooks.
Hybrid (WRD-Assam) — A multi-agency blended forecast prepared by the Water Resources Department combining inputs from multiple models for improved local accuracy.
Radar — Global precipitation radar mosaic courtesy of RainViewer.
